la niña

Styling and creative direction for the dance piece created by Aurora Vallejo, La Niña. For her final project at the Institut del Teatre, Aurora and I researched together the aesthetic language that shaped her choreographic piece. Through this moodboard — exploring references such as Persona, Kate Bush, Issey Miyake, and The Strange Case of Angelica — we aimed to establish the visual foundations around which the presentation of the piece revolved.

The work explores the relationship between mother and daughter. In the initial explorations, Aurora appears behind a fence, whose weight and symbolism are strongly present throughout the piece. Later, we see Aurora on stage, playing with the shadows created by projections of her mother.
