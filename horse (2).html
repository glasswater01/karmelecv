but mama i am a horse!!!

Styling and visual direction for a dance piece developed in collaboration with the choreographer María Landaribar, whose initial interest was to explore sound, foley, and the physicality of listening.

Drawing from references such as Piaffe and Memoria, I developed the visual universe of the piece, shaping both its atmosphere and the performer's appearance through styling and image direction. The project moved between sensory tension and intimacy, translating sound into texture, gesture, and presence.

creative direction

Through a moodboard informed by references such as Ryuichi Sakamoto: seeing sound, hearing time exhibition at the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo, we explored atmospheres suspended between tension and stillness, where the body becomes both a receiver and producer of sound. I worked on the styling and visual identity of the performer, constructing a presence that felt tactile, uncanny, and emotionally charged.

The images presented first document the physical and performative dimension of the piece, while the moodboard reveals the visual research behind it — a process of connecting cinema, texture, silence, and sensory memory to shape the world of the performance.
