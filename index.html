barcelona

Visual researcher and creative practitioner working across art direction, photography, and contemporary visual culture, with a growing focus on curatorial practice. Project Manager at Filmin and co-coordinator of the curatorial and workshop space Amonite Mur-Mur in Horta-Guinardó. Her work investigates the intersection between contemporary art and philosophy, exploring how image, technology, and sensory stimulus construct and transform perception.
